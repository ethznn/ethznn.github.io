Snowliner Support

From your first turn to backing up your progress. Find help for your ride.

Snow, wind and your own pace

Ride without music and listen to the snow and wind. If you prefer your own music, start it in another app before opening Snowliner.

If game sounds overpower your music, lower the sound level in Snowliner Settings. The iPhone side buttons adjust the overall playback volume.

Music plays through your chosen music app. Snowliner does not provide songs or a music streaming service.

Your first ride

Start your first slope challenge from Home on Sunny Slope. Earn silver to open the next course. Free Ride is a separate mode without a score target. Optional control practice teaches the basics. Skip it or retry the current step from Pause; repeat it from Settings → Help.

Tilt and stance

Hold your phone comfortably in landscape and wait until ready. Lower one end slightly to turn that way. Tilt forward to tuck, or back and hold to brake. Use the Brake button when fore/aft input is off. To switch your leading foot, flick the top of the phone toward you and return to neutral during stable riding. Pressing the sides of the screen does not add steering in tilt mode.

Touch and calibration

Choose touch in Settings → Controls if tilting is difficult. Move the pad sideways to turn, up to tuck and down to brake. The 180° button switches your leading foot. Recalibrate if your neutral position changes. Leaving the app or changing screen orientation pauses the ride for preparation.

Medals and courses

Collect bronze, silver and gold on 11 regular courses: 33 medals in total. Course medals and new courses are earned in Line Challenge. Finish for bronze. Reach the displayed silver score with at most two falls, or the higher gold score with no falls, in one finished run. Free Ride does not award course medals, but its finishes count toward record medals. The challenge screen shows the exact score target and course unlocks.

Gates and bonuses

Pass through a numbered gate's ground circle for 100 base points. Collect gates in order to increase the chain bonus by 25, up to 100 extra points per gate. Collect all four gates in one section for 400 more points. Missing a gate or falling ends the chain; earned points remain. See the breakdown on the result screen. Retry the same line to keep its layout. Selecting a new challenge generates a new layout. Personal best comparisons refer to the same line.

Minimap, falls and finish

The arrow shows position and travel direction, the circle marks the start, and the flag marks the finish. The pale line continues beyond the finish. After crossing, falling or reaching a displayed speed of zero automatically opens the results and saves the run. Before the finish, wait for the rider to stand, then release the controls or return to neutral to recover.

Reading the balance circle

The dot shows the game rider's displacement from a supported posture, not the phone's angle. A deep but stable turn can keep the dot near the center. The short trail shows recent movement; the rim marker shows the direction of continued drift. Green means returning toward support. Both sideways and fore/aft movement are represented. Edge slips and repeated sharp turns may appear as separate warnings. This is a game indicator, not a measurement or diagnosis of your physical balance.

Recovering balance

Repeated large left-right movements leave the rider swaying. Danger warns of a chance to recover; it is not a timer that guarantees a fall. Return the phone to your starting posture and hold it steady to let the sway settle. A small correction in the indicated direction or braking can settle it sooner. Holding the phone still while tilted keeps steering, so it is different from holding neutral. Once the rider has lost support and a fall has begun, that fall cannot be cancelled. Return to neutral and give the rider a moment instead of throwing the phone from side to side.

Medal collection and records

Keep 24 recent lines, including up to eight favorites protected from automatic cleanup. The oldest ordinary records are removed first. Deleting a line keeps medals and personal bests. Open Medal Collection from Home or My Records to see earned and locked medals and their requirements. Six and twelve course medals unlock board colors; all 33 unlock a completion card. Appearance does not affect performance. Existing medals and unlocked courses are preserved.

Record medals

Collect 12 more medals: finish 1, 5, 10, 25, 50 and 100 regular-course runs, and earn cumulative challenge scores of 1,000, 5,000, 10,000, 25,000, 50,000 and 100,000. Free Ride and Line Challenge finishes both count; control practice does not. Only finished challenges add points. Totals remain when lines are deleted or trimmed. Older scores start from retained line records; existing finish counts are preserved. Record medals do not unlock courses or board colors.

Backup and storage

Use Settings → Help → Progress Backup to export or restore a file. It includes medals, unlocked courses and line records, but not control, sound or display settings. Review the contents before restoring; you can return to the progress saved just before the last file restore. Test builds accept backups from the same play mode only. There is no account or automatic cloud sync. Save a file outside the app before switching devices or deleting the app. If saving fails, free storage and choose Save Again. Automatic recovery from a damaged save may omit the latest run.

My record ghost

Time trials are available on every home-screen course after it is unlocked. Control practice remains separate. Pass all required gates shown on the preparation screen to save your first valid finish and each faster best automatically. Show or hide the ghost in preparation or pause. New bests are recorded even when hidden.

Only matching courses, boards and riding rules can replay together. The clock stops at the finish line. Preparation and pauses are excluded; falls and recovery are included.

Best times and ghosts are stored separately for each course and board, and compared only on the same layout. Ghosts stay on this device and survive album deletion. Clear them in Settings → Help → Clear ghost files; medals and best times remain. Manual backups exclude replay files. After clearing or changing devices, set a new best to record a new ghost. Old ride lines cannot generate ghosts.

Gate edges

The entire visible ground circle is the target, with a small edge allowance. Fast crossings are checked along the path between frames. Time trials mark a miss only after you pass the whole circle. Turning back or a recovery relocation cannot fill a missed required gate. When gates change, old records are kept separately from the new best and ghost. Nearby ghosts keep their outline; their body fades further only when it occludes your rider.

Snow, wind and your own music

Ride without music and listen to the wind and the board carving through snow. If you prefer your own music, start it in another app before opening Snowliner. Adjust game volume separately in Settings; this does not change music volume in other apps. Snowliner does not include songs or a music streaming service.

1.1.0 — Free lifts, or ride without limits

Start 3 free rides daily, shared across all regular courses and modes. Practice is unlimited. Once free rides run out, choose to watch a rewarded ad and depart on your selected course. Buy the Unlimited Lift Pass once for unlimited rides without rewarded ads. Previous paid-app purchasers keep unlimited access at no extra cost. Courses unlock through medals.

A lift is used when you depart. Restarting from the top uses another; resuming a paused ride does not. Free lifts reset to 3 at 00:00 UTC and do not accumulate. Ad rewards cannot be stockpiled: after the ad, confirm your riding posture and depart. Ads and purchases require internet access. No separate account is required.
