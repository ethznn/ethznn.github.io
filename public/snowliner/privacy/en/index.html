Snowliner is an offline snowboard game with no account. The game does not send personal information or play records to a developer-operated server. It contains no advertising, tracking, advertising identifiers or third-party analytics SDK.

On-device processing

Device motion is used for tilt controls. Ride times, turns, falls, virtual in-game paths, challenges and control, sound and language preferences are stored locally. The app does not request GPS location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo-library access.

Sharing

Only when you choose Share is a game image passed to the iOS share sheet. Any app or service you then choose handles it under its own policy. The temporary sharing image is replaced on the next share and may be cleaned up by the operating system.

Storage and deletion

The album holds up to 24 lines, including up to 8 favorites. Individual lines can be deleted in the album; goals and personal bests are retained separately. Deletions are included in subsequent saves. A recovery backup may contain the previous saved state. Removing the app removes its local records and settings. Device backups follow your operating-system settings and Apple's policy. No separate cloud synchronization is provided.

Purchases and support

Apple handles app purchases, payment and refunds. The developer does not receive payment-card details. Use App Support on the App Store listing for help. Contact details and content you voluntarily send by email may be used to respond to your request.

Development and test builds

Development builds may keep temporary motion and frame diagnostics on the device. They are not sent automatically. Normal distribution builds do not create these diagnostic records.

Changes

If data handling changes, this policy will be updated in the app and on the support website.

File backup and restore

Progress is exported through Files only when you choose to do so. The file contains medals, unlocked courses and in-game routes and records. If you select a cloud provider, its policies apply. Deleting the app does not delete files saved outside it. Restoring replaces current progress; a separate local copy preserves progress from just before the last file restore. Temporary exports are replaced on the next export. Temporary imported files are removed after inspection.

Ghost replay records

Your first valid finish and faster personal bests in supported time trials store timestamps, in-game positions and poses locally, even while ghosts are hidden. Raw device sensor readings and real-world locations are not recorded in replays. Each replay is limited to 1 MiB, with at most 21 files and 32 MiB total. Clear ghost files in Settings → Help. They are separate from album deletion and excluded from manual progress backups. There is no automatic upload or friend sharing.

About this support website

These documents are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. This is separate from the app’s on-device record handling. We have not added analytics, advertising or contact forms to this website.
